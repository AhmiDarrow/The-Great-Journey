# Roadmap

The Great Journey: a light Warrior Cats RPG for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge). It is multiplayer playable.

## Built (unreleased)
- **Clanlands, the book worlds at grand scale.**
  - The lake territories surround spawn: a 600–800 block lake, territories 800–1,050 blocks deep, the Gathering island and its tree-bridge, the Moonpool in the hills, the horseplace, and half-bridges.
  - The old forest is 10–12.5 km to the south-east: Fourtrees, the Thunderpath, the river with a waterfall into a sheer gorge and another out of it, Sunningrocks, Highstones and Mothermouth, Tallpines, the Twolegplace, Barley's farm and Carrionplace.
  - Each seed varies every shape and spot.
  - No giant mountains: the drama comes from ravines, gorges and waterfalls.
- **The tunnels.**
  - Three entrances: by the border stream, above the stone hollow, and on the WindClan moor.
  - A cavern with a dark stream, and glowrocks to light the way.
  - The tunnel keeper: a sharp-tongued ThunderClan medicine cat beside the Ancient Stick.
- **Clanlife.**
  - The four lake Clans, with generated cats, camps, nests, fresh-kill piles and territory claims.
  - The StarClan dream tutorial: Bluestar, Lionheart, Spottedleaf and Yellowfang.
  - Three starts: kittypet, join a Clan, loner. Patrol encounters and camp invitations; the apprentice ceremony.
- **Clan events.** Badgers, foxes and eagles, border raids, injuries and sickness (seasonal), kits, warrior ceremonies, hunting patrols, a lost kit. Deaths happen as in the books without gore: vigils, the StarClan memory, and a leader's nine lives with succession.
- **StarClan's Guidance.** The guide book, told by book cats.
- **Clanwild.** Speed strike, the Alex's Mobs / Naturalist overlap, and prey glue.
- **Pack.**
  - Configs rebuilt from mod defaults plus documented choices.
  - Off-theme mods cut.
  - Season names: newleaf, greenleaf, leaf-fall and leaf-bare.

- **Campaign and Free Mode.**
  - The title screen offers Campaign, Free Mode and Load World.
  - Campaign: the dream tutorial, the Gathering at Fourtrees, then kittypet, Clan or loner starts in the old forest; patrols find kittypets and loners.
  - Four acts: the forest felled, Midnight's message, the Great Journey (highway, farm, pass, the Tribe), the lake.
- **The Great Journey route.** The highway, Twoleg farm, mountain pass, the Tribe's cave, Midnight's sett and the ridge over the lake are placed in each world, with the Tribe, Midnight, Purdy and the pass eagles settled at them.
- **Monsters on the Thunderpaths.** Block-display cars and trucks that growl, roar past and hurt what they hit; configurable.
- **Camps.** One way in and a defensible barrier each; predators and monsters kept out by the sanctuary.
- **Guided tour.** `/greatjourney tour`.
- **Test tools.** `inspect_world.py`, `playtest.py` and the region renderers and scanners (see [clanlands.md](clanlands.md)).

## In progress
- **RPG dialogue window** (portrait, typewriter text, choices) and the **journal** (tasks, duties, Clan, places, StarClan).
- **Quests and duties.**
  - Talk to Clan cats.
  - Optional duties: the medicine cat's herbs, hunting and border patrols, elders' moss, queens and kits, Clan-flavoured hunting.
  - The apprentice story path, ending in a warrior ceremony where the player chooses their name.

## Built since
- **Gatherings at every full moon** and the medicine cats' half-moon meeting, in Free Mode and the Campaign.
- **Rising through the Clan:** an apprentice of your own, deputy, leader and nine lives; a Clan of your own in Free Mode.
- **Orders:** a deputy or leader sends patrols out, or leads one.
- **Free Mode:** Moonpool dreams (StarClan's visits, with cats fallen in this world), kittypets, loners and rogues to talk to, and loners' favours for cats of no Clan.
