The Great Journey
=================

A Warrior Cats modpack for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge). Every world is built around the
Clan territories from the books: the lake territories where you start, and the old forest
with Fourtrees a long journey away. Four living Clans, a StarClan dream to learn the ways
of the warrior, and three ways to begin: kittypet, Clan apprentice or loner.

Configuration
-------------
Every config this pack ships is the mod's own default with a few documented changes
(listed, with reasons, in the pack's config-choices.json). Mods not listed run on their
own defaults.
