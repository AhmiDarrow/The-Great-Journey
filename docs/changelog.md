# Changelog

## 0.1.8

- **Every quest is on your Clan map.** The camp you are looking for, each point of a border patrol (numbered), the cat to take a finished task back to, the walk to the Moonpool for nine lives, land where you could found a Clan, and tonight's Gathering or half-moon meeting all have their mark. The quest you are following is the gold star; the others are pale blue. Your story's step is followed until you choose another: press **Show on map** on a task's journal page, or click a mark on the big map.
- **See your quest's cats on the map.** While it is going on, the map shows your apprentice, your mentor while they train you, the cats walking on your patrol, trespassers you have come upon at the border and a friend you have spoken with across it, as long as they are near enough to be loaded. An orange "!" marks where your Clan's latest event happened, for a few minutes.
- **A better big map.** Zoom with the mouse wheel and drag to move about. You can open it straight from the world: give **Open the Clan Map** a key in Options, Controls (it has none until you do).
- **Your journal says what a task is worth.** Each task shows its reward, and once there is nothing left to do it says so and tells you whom to take it back to. The Clan page shows what your standing opens: at 40 trespassing patrols heed your warning, at 60 StarClan calls you trusted, at 120 you may be named deputy, and at 200 you may lead.
- **Prey you hand in feeds your Clan.** Prey you bring for a task now goes onto the fresh-kill pile, where your Clanmates eat it, and in leaf-bare it keeps the elders, queens and kits well. If the pile is full, what will not fit stays with you. A founded Clan's hungry day now asks you to bring two pieces home.
- **Storytellers keep their own pace.** Every storyteller used to settle to about one event every ten minutes for a Clan. Now Spottedleaf spaces them out, Tigerstar presses harder, and Midnight keeps a long calm and then sends them one on another's heels, with one warning for the whole storm.
- **Fixed: the friend across the border and the wanderer at your camp can be talked to.** Greeting them with an empty paw got you the answer meant for a cat of another Clan, so a friendship could not go on and a wanderer whose question you closed left unanswered.
- **Fixed: a founded Clan's leader can send patrols out.** "Take a patrol round our territory" and "Take a hunting patrol out" did nothing in a Clan you founded.
- **Fixed: a loner's favour can always be handed in,** even after you have joined or founded a Clan. It used to stay in your journal for good.
- **Fixed: StarClan asks about the Dark Forest once.** A cat who chose to keep training was asked, and charged, at every dream, and could not ask for another storyteller. Later dreams now offer the way back among the other choices.
- **Fixed: no cat dies in a border fight.** Your own Clanmates could be killed by trespassers. Only grown warriors join in now, and clawing the trespassers starts the fight even if you never chose it.
- **Fixed: warnings and prophecies are not lost.** One that came due as you reached camp could vanish before its cats had appeared. A foretold fire comes even if the storm has passed. A prophecy's "two or three days" now counts the nights you sleep through.
- **Fixed: Midnight's storm.** What she warns of now comes first, before the rest of her storm.
- **Fixed: Spottedleaf protects a Clan you founded.** And prey that trespassers carry off is now missing from your pile even when you were far from camp.

## 0.1.7

- **Who watches over your Clans?** A new Free Mode world asks you which cat will watch over it, and they shape what happens there. **Spottedleaf** is gentle: few attacks, more kits and ceremonies, and no cat dies of anything but old age. **Bluestar** is the books as written. **Yellowfang** brings hard seasons, with sickness, injuries and a leaf-bare that bites. **Tigerstar** brings war, and sends more of it the stronger you grow. **Midnight** keeps you waiting, then sends everything at once. Existing worlds are watched by Bluestar. Dedicated servers choose with `newWorldStoryteller` in `clanlands-common.toml`.
- **Omens.** Before a dangerous event, your Clan's medicine cat has a sign (if you are the medicine cat, it comes to you). You have a little time to get ready, less under Tigerstar. Spottedleaf says plainly what is coming; Midnight never does.
- **The world gives you room to breathe.** After a cat is lost, trouble stays away for a while, except under Tigerstar. After a long quiet, something comes.
- **Your storyteller visits your dreams** at the Moonpool and speaks of how you have lived. There you can ask for another to watch over the Clans; everyone playing in the world is told. Operators can use `/greatjourney quest storyteller <name>`.
- **Free Mode: StarClan speaks at the Moonpool.** Come to the Moonpool by night, as any cat, and you dream. A cat who has fallen in your world comes to you, one of your own Clan if any has. StarClan gives you a sign: the way to a place of the books you have not found yet. You wake healed, with a little experience. StarClan speaks to each cat once a moon (eight nights).
- **Prophecies.** At the Moonpool, StarClan may give your Clan a riddle that comes true two or three days later. Puzzle it out with your medicine cat (one guess a day). If you are right in time, your Clan is ready: a foreseen sickness passes you by, and warriors who were warned fight harder. The riddle is in your journal.
- **Border skirmishes.** Out in your territory you may come on another Clan's patrol, hunting on your side. Warn them off (they listen to a deputy, a leader, a cat leading a patrol, a trusted warrior, or one with Clanmates at their side), drive them off with claws, or let them take what they caught. No cat dies in these fights: a beaten cat runs. Your patrol fights beside you. Prey carried off is missing from your Clan's fresh-kill pile, and both Clans have the news at the next Gathering.
- **The warrior code.** Out in your Clan's land you will now and then meet a hard choice, as the books' cats do: a starving loner with your Clan's mouse, a lost kit from across the border, a rival drowning in the stream, a blame that should be yours. Neither answer is easy. What your Clan thinks of you and what StarClan thinks of your honour do not always move the same way. Your storyteller remembers.
- **Leaf-bare hunger.** In leaf-bare, your Clan lives on what is really in its fresh-kill pile. Every day the elders, queens and kits eat from it first. If it runs short, one of them falls sick and hunters go out, so keep it full. Your journal shows what the pile holds and what it needs. Yellowfang's winters are hungrier; under Spottedleaf only a bare pile makes a cat ill.
- **The Dark Forest.** Under Tigerstar, or if you break the code often, a shadowy tabby may visit your sleep and offer to train you. The training is real: experience, and a day of stronger claws. So is the price. After a few nights StarClan asks you about it at the Moonpool: turn away from him, or keep training and walk without StarClan's signs. You can still turn back later.
- **A friend across the border.** Near a border you may meet a cat of another Clan. Stay and talk and you may meet again. A Clanmate may notice, and you will have to tell the truth or lie. A friend you have met often will one day ask you for something that puts friendship against your Clan.
- **A deputy commands patrols, not single cats.** A deputy or a leader can now talk to a warrior and say "I have orders for you". Send a patrol of up to three cats round the territory or out to hunt, and they go and come home by themselves, on Warrior Cats' own patrols. Show one cat exactly where to patrol on Warrior Cats' patrol map. Or bring a patrol of up to four and lead it yourself: they walk two by two around you, fight what you fight, and all wait, follow or go home when you tell any one of them. Sending the patrols out earns the Clan's trust, once a day.
- **Your apprentice keeps up.** An apprentice at your side who falls behind now runs to you, and one left far behind is brought to you, even across land that has unloaded. Asked along once, they fall in beside you again by themselves after you die, sleep or come back to the game. The warriors on a patrol with you keep up the same way.
- **Kittypets, loners and rogues talk.** Greet a cat of no Clan with an empty paw, the same way as a Clanmate. Kittypets and loners tell you about their lives, what they know of the Clans, and the latest news from the Clan whose land you are standing on. Each has their own way of talking, and answers a Clan cat differently from a kittypet or a loner. Rogues only threaten.
- **A life for cats of no Clan.** A kittypet or loner with no Clan had nothing to do but look for one. Now a loner you talk to may ask a favour, once a day each: catch prey for them, or look over a stretch of land. It goes in your journal like a Clan duty, and you hand it in by talking to them again. It earns experience and standing that you keep when you join a Clan, or found one. The journal tells a Clanless cat about both.
- **A Clan of your own, alive.** While you are among your own cats: a wanderer asks to join, a kit is born, a cat falls sick, a stray dog comes sniffing round, or your Clan goes hungry and asks you to hunt. Your storyteller weighs these too. A founded Clan grows by these to 16 cats.
- **Quiet things in camp.** "Sit with me a while" on a Clanmate: share tongues (it heals a little, and your Clan notices once a day), ask an elder for a tale (the elders tell what has really happened in your world, and name your fallen), or sit vigil for a Clanmate you lost, on that night or the day after.
- **StarClan's Guidance** has new pages: Giving Orders and Cats of No Clan in "Life in a Clan", and Dreams in "The Island and the Moonpool".
- **Smaller fixes.** A conversation with many choices now fits on the screen.

## 0.1.6

- **A named cat is treated as one.** Once you have your warrior name, your Clanmates greet you as a warrior, a deputy, a leader, a medicine cat or an elder, and ask a warrior's work of you. No elder calls a deputy "young one" or sends them for moss, and no warrior sets them to practise the hunter's crouch.
- **A deputy leads the patrols.** A deputy had no deputy to ask, and so had no patrols at all. Now your leader gives you the border patrol and the hunting patrol to lead, and the warriors nearby fall in behind you. A leader hears from their own deputy where the Clan needs them.
- **More to do.** Every Clanmate now has something to ask, once a day each. The leader sends a warrior they trust to scout a place in the territory. The deputy and the medicine cat may want a place looked over. An elder asks how a place they remember looks these days. Warriors offer a hunting race or a climbing dare, or ask for a share of your prey after a long patrol. Apprentices ask a warrior to show them how to stalk and where the prey runs, and race another apprentice. Kits want a moss-ball, or to see a real mouse up close.
- **Your apprentice always gets their name.** Your apprentice now stays at your side until you tell them to wait in camp (they used to leave after twenty minutes without a word, and the lessons stopped counting). One left behind in the territory, because you died, left the game or went on without them, comes home to camp by itself. The naming is held as soon as you are both in camp. The journal says when to ask them along, and why you have no apprentice yet.
- **The Clan no longer takes your apprentice for its medicine cat.** A Clan with no medicine cat took its first apprentice, even one you were training, and left them a "-paw" for good. It also did this whenever its own medicine cat was only out gathering herbs. Now it names a new one only when it truly has none, never your apprentice, and the apprentice it chooses takes a full name. Medicine cats left as "-paw" in existing worlds are given their full names.
- **A cat born in the nursery is named once.** An apprentice who grew up in the nursery was given a warrior name at their ceremony and then a different one when they finished growing. They now finish growing at the ceremony and keep one name.
- **A named cat keeps their rank.** A cat who had earned their name and joined a Clan again was made an apprentice, with a mentor and no way on. They now join as a warrior or a medicine cat, and a world where this had already happened is put right. A named cat is always drawn full-grown.
- **Smaller fixes.** A mentor out with their apprentice is still called to the Gathering. In the Campaign, StarClan speaks to you by name.

## 0.1.5

- **A lost mentor is always replaced.** If your mentor dies, leaves the Clan or retires, you are told at once wherever you are, and the leader names a new mentor as soon as you are back in camp. What you had already done of the step carries over. In the Campaign, your mentor is still your mentor after the Clans settle at the lake.
- **An apprentice of your own.** A warrior or a full medicine cat is given one of the Clan's apprentices. Ask them along, teach them four lessons, and the leader gives them their warrior name. A medicine cat names their own apprentice.
- **Deputy and leader.** Train an apprentice and earn the Clan's trust, and your leader can make you deputy. A deputy leads the Clan when the leader's last life is gone, or when the old leader steps down for them. A new leader walks to the Moonpool for nine lives and a "-star" name, and chooses a deputy by talking to a warrior. The journal says what the next step needs.
- **Found a Clan of your own (Free Mode).** A loner can win over wild cats, lead three of them to land no Clan has marked, and found a Clan there. The journal shows what the new Clan still needs: a camp, six cats, a deputy and a medicine cat. Then its founder receives nine lives at the Moonpool, and the four Clans hear of it at their next Gathering.
- **Your warrior name is yours to choose.** At your naming ceremony you can keep the name you chose when you made your cat, take one of four your Clan offers, or ask for other names as often as you like.
- **Existing worlds load as they are.** Checked against worlds saved by 0.1.4: every cat keeps their mentor, their step and their standing.

## 0.1.4

- **Many more warrior names.** Warrior names now end in one of 231 endings in the books' style (-pelt, -stripe, -frost, -flight, -whisker, -fang, -song, -blossom, -pool and many more), so the Clans can have tens of thousands of different warriors. Your own warrior ceremony offers four new endings in every world, and no name uses the same word twice (no "Firefire").

## 0.1.3

- **Worlds keep opening.** Three worldgen mods together made the world's saved terrain settings grow every time the world was opened: one part tripled, until after about a dozen opens the world refused to load ("Tried to read NBT tag that was too big"); another added a layer each time, slowing new terrain a little more on every open. Both now stay the same size, and a world already hit by this opens again and shrinks back on its next save.

## 0.1.2

- **Slower seasons.** Each season now lasts 72 days instead of 24, and the leaves change colour every 24 days instead of every 8.

## 0.1.1: the first release

Fixes from the family playtest, on top of everything below:

- **Act 2 reaches the sea.** Walking anywhere onto the beach where the sun drowns (or to Midnight's sett) now counts, the journal and a reminder tell you how far and which way the sea is, and StarClan's sign comes after a few days even if you were away from the forest.
- **Mentors are shared out.** A new apprentice, or one whose mentor has gone to StarClan, gets a warrior who has no apprentice yet whenever the Clan has one.
- **Livelier camps.** Each cat does a few different things through the day, and the morning hunting patrol no longer gets skipped on busy days.

## 0.1.0

The Great Journey: live as a Warrior Cat among the four Clans, from the old forest to the lake.

- **Two ways to play.** Campaign follows the books: life in the old forest, the Twolegs tearing it down, StarClan's sign, Midnight by the sea where the sun drowns, the Great Journey over the mountains, and the new home by the lake. Free Mode drops you into Clan life by the lake.
- **The Clan lands.** Every world builds the books' places from its seed: Fourtrees, the four camps, Sunningrocks, Highstones, Twolegplace, the Thunderpath, the island, the Moonpool, the Tribe's cave behind the waterfall, and more.
- **The Clan map.** Open your Clan Journal (J) and turn to the Map bookmark. It shows the lake, the old forest or the whole Great Journey, with every camp and place named, a key, where you are, and a gold star on where your story is sending you. Click the map to see it big.
- **Clan life.** Apprentice and warrior duties, medicine cat work, patrols, Gatherings, ceremonies, scars, seasons and Clan news.
- **A dedicated server pack** is attached to this file: unzip it, run install, then start.
