# Clanlands: the book worlds

Clanlands builds every new world around the Clan territories from the Warriors books. Canon facts and layout ranges are in [book-geography.md](book-geography.md).

## What a world gets
- **The lake territories at spawn.**
  - An irregular lake with the Gathering island and its fallen-tree bridge.
  - ShadowClan pines (north-west), ThunderClan woods (north-east), RiverClan marsh and streams with the outflow river (south-west), WindClan moor (south-east).
  - The horseplace between RiverClan and WindClan.
  - The Moonpool at the head of the ThunderClan/WindClan border stream, in the hills to the north-east.
  - New players start on the ThunderClan shore by the Sky Oak.
- **The old forest, 8–10 km to the south-east.**
  - Fourtrees at the centre, with four great oaks and the Great Rock.
  - The Thunderpath between ShadowClan and ThunderClan.
  - The river: off WindClan's moor in a thirty-block waterfall into a deep rock gorge, then out across the plains past Sunningrocks and the RiverClan island camp.
  - WindClan's high moor to the west.
  - Further out: Tallpines and the Twolegplace, Barley's farm, and Highstones with Mothermouth and the Moonstone.
- **Everywhere else**, the pack's normal world. No giant mountains.

## Seeds
Every size, angle, border wiggle, shoreline, river bend and landmark spot comes from the world seed, through a named stream per value (`Geo.Rand`). The same seed always rebuilds the same map; two seeds never match. The books' arrangement is fixed: which Clan is where, who holds which landmark, and the lake-to-forest direction.

## How it works
- **Plan:** `layout/LakeLands` and `layout/OldForest` answer, for any column, the zone, the planned ground height, how strongly the map applies (1 inside, fading to 0 over 200 blocks at the edge) and the terrain smoothness.
- **Terrain:** `world/WorldgenPatcher` rewrites the overworld's references to vanilla's surface offset, jaggedness and factor as the data packs load, blending them toward the plan by its weight. Caves, ores, aquifers and surface rules stay vanilla.
- **Biomes:** `mixin/MultiNoiseBiomeSourceMixin` returns each zone's biome above ground inside the maps. Zones use existing pack biomes (first one installed wins), so spawns, colours, seasons and decoration behave normally. Wild Clan cats are enabled there through Warrior Cats Events' `mod_spawn_biomes` tag.
- **Landmarks:** `landmark/Landmarks` is one feature that runs once per chunk at the end of decoration and paints the part of each landmark that falls in that chunk. Each landmark draws its shape from its own seed. It runs only in the Clan lands' own overworld.
- **Streams and falls:** `landmark/Waters` digs each channel above sea level to its planned bed and fills it to the planned surface, never above the banks beside it. At each step down it finds the real brink (the noise moves it a few blocks from the plan) and hangs a curtain of falling water there. A river stretch that starts at the foot of another's fall is marked `continuing()`, so the lower stretch never wears back the lip of the fall above it.
- **Roads:** Thunderpaths are graded to their planned bed. Where the bed runs more than six blocks over the ground (a ravine, a stream's gully) or over water, the road becomes a stone bridge with low walls and a pier every twelve blocks, never an earth dam. Culverts run on as open cuts until the land is low, so both mouths open. Clearing above a road keeps any block that holds a stream's water.
- **Lava:** `landmark/Lava` turns all lava from y 50 up inside the Clan lands to stone, and every bit of lava around the tunnels down to below the cavern floor. Lava in deep ordinary caves is left alone.
- **Safe camps:** `world/CampSanctuary` stops natural and worldgen spawns of monsters and of the `clanlands:camp_predators` tag (foxes, wolves, badgers, eagles and similar) inside every camp. Warrior Cats Events makes foxes attack cats, and taiga foxes spawning on the ShadowClan camp floor killed its cats on the first load. The Clans' own fox and badger events still happen.
- **Clan events** (`life/events/ClanEvents`, `Happenings`): only a camp that is live (its creatures moving, so within a player's simulation distance) has events; one merely loaded at the edge of view had its cats frozen, and its badger was announced but never came (Kiki, 2026-10-18). The badger starts just outside the way in and is steered in along it to the nursery; it never appears on the camp's rim, which over a hollow or a ravine is the cliff top. An event's fox starts outside the way in and its eagle above the camp; both carry `greatjourney_event_foe` and leave after two minutes (one that unloads meanwhile is removed when it loads again).
- **Tunnels:** a tunnel never drops more than 0.7 blocks a block, so a cat can walk back up. Where the real ground stands over a mouth, a ramp is cut back out of it. The stream-side mouth sits on the bank just outside the stream's ravine, on the cavern's side.
- **Camps** (`landmark/CampPlan` plans them, `landmark/Camps` paints them): every camp has one way in, a natural barrier everywhere else, and dens by what they are for. The Clan's cats are settled into their own dens (nursery for queens and kits, warriors' den for the deputy and warriors...) from the same plan, so nests and dens always line up. Every den has a fixed set of nests, each on its own block of the den's floor, kept bare by the painter and given a moss bed whether or not a cat sleeps there (`CampPlan.nests`: warriors eight, nursery and apprentices four, elders three, medicine two, leader one; WindClan's warriors' burrow is a little wider to hold them). A player who joins a Clan gets a nest of their own in the apprentices' den and wakes there (`life/Nests`, one per player), moves to a warriors'-den nest at the warrior ceremony, and in the Campaign moves with the Clan to the lake camp (if online when it settles). Camps filled before every den had its set get the missing beds once, the first time their middle chunk loads. Lake ThunderClan's stone hollow: sheer quarry walls, a cut climbing the wall to a thorn tunnel, Highledge with the leader's cave behind it and tumbled rocks up to it, the elders in honeysuckle beneath, the medicine cleft with its pool behind a bramble screen, a bramble nursery. Old-forest ThunderClan: a bramble-and-gorse ring at the foot of a rocky ravine, the gorse tunnel at the bottom of the ravine path, Highrock with the leader's den at its foot, Spottedleaf's fern tunnel to a cleft in a great rock, apprentices by a stump, elders under a fallen tree. WindClan: a hollow ringed by gorse with one gap, Tallrock with climbing stones, every den a burrow dug into the hollow's sides. RiverClan: a wall of bramble and willow lined with reeds where it meets water (the water is the wall where it reaches the camp); the old forest's island is made dry ground with a ford of stones to the nearest bank; willows lean over. ShadowClan: a dense bramble wall with one tunnel, tall pines in the wall keeping it dim, bramble-thicket dens, the elders under a fallen pine's roots, the leader in a hollow under a great rock; the old forest's is marshy with puddles. Gates face easy ground (no stream, ravine or road just outside); old-forest camps are moved clear of the river. `CampGameTests` checks one way in, an open middle and fitting dens on six seeds.
- **Territory names:** the action bar names the territory as a player crosses into it.

## How a world is played
The title screen's Singleplayer button becomes **Campaign** and **Free Mode** side by side, with **Load World** (the usual world list) under them; Realms is removed. Campaign and Free Mode open the normal create-world screen with the mode remembered, and the world keeps it for good (`life/Mode`). Dedicated servers, and worlds made without a button, use `newWorldMode` in `clanlands-common.toml` (`free` by default). Worlds from before modes existed are Free.

- **Free Mode:** the Clans already live at the lake. A new cat has the StarClan dream, then picks a start: a kittypet at the forest edge, join a Clan, or a loner by the lake.
  - **Seeking a Clan** (`life/Starts`): a kittypet or loner is asked "Which Clan's cats will you seek?" on arriving (or "None yet"), again about once a game day while still clanless, from the ThunderClan patrol that finds a kittypet ("I seek another Clan"), and any time with `/greatjourney seek`. A chosen Clan sends a guide: a new warrior of that Clan steps out near the player and offers to lead the way. It walks them round the lake (never across it), in by the camp's one way in, and hands them to the deputy's usual invitation. The journal shows "Find the <Clan> camp" with how far and which way the camp lies. A guide turned down ("Not now") or left behind comes again after three minutes; walking to the camp alone works too. A seeking cat is not ambushed by the ThunderClan patrol. The Campaign keeps its own patrols and does not offer this. A Free Mode loner who wanders 64 blocks from the shore into a Clan's land is found by that Clan's patrol and asked to join, the way a kittypet is in ThunderClan's woods (each Clan once a game day). Since 2026-10-18 (owner) choosing a Clan skips the long walk: the cat arrives 12 blocks outside that camp's way in (`Starts.toCamp`), where one of its cats meets them and leads them the last steps in.
- **Campaign** (`life/campaign/`): the story in the books' order.
  1. **The dream tutorial** (`life/dream/`): the same short, skippable StarClan lesson as in Free Mode.
  2. **The Gathering at Fourtrees:** the player wakes on the rim of Fourtrees in the old forest under the full moon, with the four leaders on the Great Rock.
  3. **The three starts**, all in the old forest: a kittypet in the Twoleg nest at the forest's edge, join one of the four Clans, or a loner by the farm near Highstones. Kittypets and loners have no Clan yet: `campaign/Outsiders` watches them, and once one has wandered 64 blocks from where it began into a Clan's land, a patrol of three cats steps out and asks it to join. A kittypet's first patrol (ThunderClan, by the Twoleg garden) is the one that finds Rusty. A cat who says "not yet" is asked again after a day, by any Clan whose land it crosses. Since 2026-10-18 the Campaign's kittypet and loner are also asked "Which Clan's cats will you seek?" on arriving, and a chosen Clan sends a guide exactly as in Free Mode (`Starts.guideTick`, from `Outsiders`); a seeking cat is not stopped by other patrols, and the seek is dropped while the Clans are away on the journey. Kiki's Campaign loner was never asked.
  4. **The four acts**, which belong to the world (each step happens once): the old forest, then its end (Twoleg monsters fell it stretch by stretch, StarClan sends a sign, a cat hears Midnight), then the Great Journey (highway, farm, mountain pass, the Tribe of Rushing Water, the lake), then the lake (new camps, the Moonpool, the first Gathering on the island), after which play is free. Chapters belong to each player, and a player who joins late starts at the chapter that fits the world.
  - A cat who arrives while the Clans are away on the journey has one way to begin: walk with them. Once the Clans live at the lake, the same three starts begin there.
  - **Errands along the way** (`quests/StoryTasks`, `quests/Errands`): at four points of the story every Clan cat gets an optional task, a warrior's or a medicine cat's, worth the same (6 standing and 40 experience, 8 and 60 for the last). The forest's end: feed a hungry Clan (catch 4 prey, bring 3 home to camp), or save the herbs (4 each of two herbs, home to the medicine den). The journey sets out: hunt on the way (3 prey), or traveling herbs (2 each of sorrel, burnet, chamomile and daisy). The mountains: scout the pass (two points on the way to the Tribe's cave), or tend Clanmates on the journey who are shivering with chills (WCE's own disease; 3 juniper berries if none walks near). The new home: mark the new borders (a border patrol of the lake territory), or stock the new medicine den (3 each of two herbs and 4 moss), each done in the new camp. One errand is under way at a time; each is given once, and it finishes by itself (no giver to go back to) once its objectives are done, taking what it asked for. Nothing in the story waits on one, and the forest's errand is set aside when the Clans leave. The story also speaks to a medicine cat: the chapter titles and goals in the journal ("A medicine cat of the old forest"), a word from Spottedleaf in StarClan's sign, a Tribe healer who gives juniper for the cold (a warrior meets a cave-guard instead), Spottedleaf at the Moonpool, and the medicine cats sitting together at the first Gathering. Both paths end where the story ends.
  - `/greatjourney campaign` has `status`, `join`, `wake` and (ops) `act` for testing.
- Planned, not built: recurring Gatherings at the full moon in Free Mode, and Moonpool dreams. In the Campaign the Moonpool and the island Gathering are one-off chapters.
- **Duties and the apprentice path** (`life/quests/`): every duty and step should take five to ten minutes. Walks are kept within `Tasks.ROUTE_CAP` (1800 blocks out and home in straight lines, about 7.5 minutes at a cat's 4 blocks a second over real ground). A border patrol is a loop out of camp to the two or three nearest border points (one of the two nearest first, so it changes day to day), at least `ROUTE_MIN` (1000) where the borders allow; the warriors the deputy sends walk it beside the player toward the next point and home, wearing the busy tag. The territory walk's far point stops short of a far edge. The places step shows the Gathering place (the island stays, however far: 11 to 19 minutes from the camps across the lake on the test seeds) plus the nearest of the Clan's own places when it adds little; in the old forest Highstones is only told of. Each cat gives one duty a game day (20 real minutes with Better Days' defaults); `QuestGameTests.dutyWalksStayWithinTenMinutes` checks the walks on the test seeds. Any prey counts toward a catch, on duties and on the apprentice path alike: the Clan's favourite prey (squirrels and mice, rabbits, fish, frogs and lizards) is only named as the best, and a hunt's hand-in takes anything in `warriorcats_events:prey` or `#minecraft:fishes`. Catches outside the Clan's own list used not to count (playtest 2026-10-19). Nobody is sent out at night (12600 to 23200, as `CampRoutine`'s night) or onto a hunt, patrol, climb, stalk or long walk in the rain (`Tasks.keptIn`): cats decline to give such a duty then, the warriors walking a border patrol turn for camp with the player and say so (and set out again once it is light or dry), and a mentor out on a step leads the apprentice home and goes on at first light. Kiki's mentor kept her on the border patrol through the night and through rainstorms (2026-10-19). `QuestGameTests.nobodyIsSentOutAtNightOrInTheRain` checks the rule.
- **Rising through the Clan** (2026-11-23, owner): four systems in `life/quests/`, each put right every second from what is true rather than from remembered steps.
  - **A lost mentor** (`Mentors.lost`, `Story.mentorLost`): the mentor is known by its id. Loaded, it is lost if dead, out of the Clan, or no longer a cat who teaches (an elder, a queen, the medicine cat for a warrior's apprentice). Not loaded, it is lost if Warrior Cats Events' Clan list has dropped it or StarClan took a cat of its name after it became this player's mentor. The apprentice is told once wherever they are; in camp `Story.assignMentor` names another (warriors first, then the deputy, then the leader; a cat of the same name made anew simply goes on) and `Story.carryOver` keeps the step's progress. `Joining` records the mentor the leader names. Clan Lore does not retire a cat who mentors a player.
  - **An apprentice of your own** (`Pupils`): a WARRIOR, DEPUTY or MEDICINE player with their name is given a Clan apprentice in camp (one with no mentor first, else the nearest, whose mentor goes back to the patrols). The cat carries the player's id (`greatjourney_pupil_of`) and Clan Lore shows the player as its mentor (`Roles.setPlayerMentor`, `ClanKeeper.taughtByPlayer`). Four lessons (`Task.Kind.LESSON`, kept in the player's `lesson` slot; progress counts only within 48 blocks of the apprentice, who comes along when asked), then the naming in camp. An apprentice that dies, leaves or is named some other way ends the pairing; a mentor away eight days loses the mark. The Clan's own warrior ceremony event skips a player's apprentice.
  - **Deputy and leader** (`Ranks`): deputy needs one apprentice trained and standing 120 (`Ranks.deputyNeeds`), by asking the leader or when the place falls empty (`Deaths.newDeputy` asks `Ranks.deputyVacancy` first). Leader needs three days as deputy and standing 200, when the leader's last life goes (`Deaths.succession` asks `Ranks.succession` first; a deputy who is offline stays deputy) or by asking (the old leader becomes an elder). `LifeData.leaderPlayer` says who leads; the player's WCE rank stays DEPUTY, because WCE's LEADER rank may manage the Clan in its own screens. Nine lives and the "-star" name come at the Moonpool (Mothermouth in the forest). A death costs a life; at none the player is an elder and the Clan's succession runs.
  - **The naming ceremony** (`Story.ceremony`): the choices are the ending the player chose in Warrior Cats Events' cat creator (`Story.ownSuffix`, shown first), four the Clan offers (`Story.offers`, seeded by world, player and page) and "Other names", which turns the page. Saves from before 0.1.4 show every player was offered the same four.
  - **Old saves**: `RisingGameTests.anOldSaveLoadsAsItWas` reads 0.1.4-shaped data, and every world under `GJ_OLD_SAVES` when that is set (copies of real worlds; never commit them).
  - **A Clan of your own** (`Founding`, Free Mode): three tame cats near the player and a site outside every territory, 300 blocks from each camp, open `/wce clan register` through `/greatjourney found`. The founder's journal then tracks a claimed camp, six Clan cats, a deputy, a medicine cat, and the nine lives. A Clan made straight from WCE's command joins the path where it stands.
- **Kits** (`life/Kits`, 2026-11, owner): a player whose Warrior Cats Events cat is a kit joins as a kit (WCE rank KIT, a nest in the nursery), not an apprentice: no mentor, no apprentice steps, no Gathering invitation. After eight minutes of play (`Kits.KIT_TICKS`, game ticks) the leader holds the apprentice ceremony once they are in camp: their cat grows to an apprentice, the name ends in "-paw", the nest moves to the apprentices' den and the path choice follows. An apprentice whose cat was still a kit and had not begun training is put back in the nursery for the rest of that time; one already training just grows. The warrior or medicine cat naming grows the cat to full size.
- **Medicine path mentors** (2026-11): a medicine cat apprentice whose medicine cat is out of camp has the Clan's medicine cat teleported back to camp to mentor them (the nearest loaded one; a cat in a Gathering scene, guiding, or the tunnel keeper is left alone), and if none of the Clan is loaded anywhere a new medicine cat appears in camp. Only a medicine cat ever mentors the medicine path. A step can be handed in to the mentor, a cat of the mentor's name, or (on the medicine path) the Clan's medicine cat now, when the cat who gave it is gone or out of reach (`Story.answers`); before, a replaced medicine cat left the apprentice unable to hand in a step.
- **The Moonpool walk** (2026-11): on the medicine path the mentor shows the Moonpool with a full scene (the half-moon meeting, sharing tongues with StarClan), then walks the apprentice home along the shore and takes the step in camp; before, it took the step at the pool and vanished home the moment the apprentice looked away. A cat leading a player stands still while the player has a scene open. Only a medicine cat holds the medicine naming ceremony: the mentor (or the Clan's medicine cat) is brought to camp for it, never the leader.
- **Story cats on the ground** (2026-11): cats that hold still for a scene (the StarClan teachers, the Campaign's opening Gathering at Fourtrees, Gathering and half-moon crowds) have no AI and so no gravity: they are now set down on the floor itself (a slab's or a bed's top, never the air over it) and never on a tree's limbs (`BookClans.rest`, and `standable` looks under branches). The teachers no longer glow (the outline showed through everything, so they looked like white shapes floating in the dark); they sit, turn their heads to the dreamer, and a few faint stars drift off their fur. The Fourtrees warriors sit facing the Great Rock, the leaders stand looking out over their Clans.
- **A place in the Clan** (`life/quests/Paths`): the apprentice ceremony ends with a choice, "Train as a warrior" or "Train as a medicine cat" (closing the window keeps the warrior's path), and talking to the leader always offers "About my place in the Clan". A warrior's apprentice can switch to the medicine cat's path and back (training restarts at the first step with a new mentor; standing is kept). A warrior (or the deputy) can retire to the elders' den or move to the nursery to help raise the kits, a medicine cat can retire, and an elder or a nursery cat can go back to what they were. Each place is the player's own Warrior Cats Events rank (MEDICINEAPP, MEDICINE, ELDER, QUEEN), and their nest moves to the matching den (`Nests.denFor`). The medicine cat's path has six steps of its own, taught by the Clan's medicine cat: the camp with the medicine den (where they now sleep), two herbs, remedies (two dock poultices and three poppy seeds, both made in the crafting grid), the elders (a look into their den, four moss and two daisy leaves), the places (the Moonpool instead of the Gathering island by the lake; in the old forest the Moonstone is told of), and a test of three herbs, two of each. The medicine cat then names them a full medicine cat in camp (the player picks the end of their name, as at the warrior ceremony). Medicine cat players are already asked along to the half-moon meeting (`gathering/HalfMoon`). A medicine cat (or medicine cat apprentice) asking "Do you need help?" gets a healer's duty instead of a warrior's (`Tasks.healerDutyFor`), worth the same: the Clan's medicine cat wants the herb store filled or the sick in camp cured, the deputy sends them to two footsore or shivering warriors, a warrior or apprentice has sore pads, an elder wants daisy leaves for their joints or poppy seeds to sleep, a queen's kit has a fever. The sick are real: `quests/Patients` picks the patients when the duty is taken on (a cat WCE has already made ill first, else one falls ill with something mild: a fever, whitecough, chills or sore pads, never greencough or deathberries), the journal names each with its cure ("Cure Ashclaw's fever (feverfew leaves)"), and the player cures them the way WCE does, a right-click with the cure. A cure counts once the cat no longer has the disease; if WCE's diseases are turned off, the duty asks for the cure's herbs instead. `HealerGameTests` checks the duties, a real fever given and cured, and the errands. Asked for in the playtest (2026-10-19): "No med cat option", and other Clan roles for a cat who does not want to be a warrior.
- **The mentor** (`life/quests/Mentor`, run from `Story`) is there for the apprentice path, not only talking. Step 1 is a real tour. The mentor leads out through the way in, to the lake shore (or the edge of Fourtrees), past one or two of the Clan's landmarks if they lie near the route, out to the far reaches and home, with a word at each place. On getting back they take the step there and then. On the hunt (step 2) the mentor stays near, gives a tip for the Clan's prey and says something after each catch. They walk the border patrol (step 4) along its route, naming the Clan beyond each border, and back to camp. They take the apprentice to the places (step 5): round the lake and over the tree-bridge to the island. They watch the assessment (step 6) from about twelve blocks off and react to each catch, or to a slip. Once a step is done and the mentor is close, they ask for it (once per step). Between steps the mentor pads over in camp for a word about the current step, at most every six minutes. A walk that is cut short is offered again 45 seconds later, when the two next meet. A mentor left far from camp goes home once out of sight, if its camp's land is loaded. Step 1 is now the camp only (owner, 2026-10-18): the way in, each den with a word at its door (in order round the camp), the leader's rock (Highledge, Highrock, Tallrock), then the middle, where the step is taken; the territory is learned on the patrol and places steps. Every cat that walks with a player (guides, mentors, patrol warriors) waits for them and is only moved where the player cannot see (`Lead.inSight`); a mentor going home after a walk does so unseen.
- **Leading** (`life/Lead`): one helper behind the guides and the mentor, per player. The cat walks 4 to 9 blocks ahead, stops and looks back when the player falls further behind (with an occasional "Keep up!"), and hurries when the player overtakes. Each stretch (up to 40 blocks) is a path worked out on the land as it stands, one the player can walk too: steps of one block up, short drops, round walls, cliffs and ravines, through water only when there is no dry way, never through fire or lava. The cat walks every step and never turns up ahead of the player on its own (Kiki's mentor led her into a marsh, then waited on top of a cliff for her to climb, 2026-10-18). A place the path can't get nearer is shown from where the cat stands if it is within the stop's reach (down a gorge, out in the water); further off, the stop is passed by with a word ("no safe way over there"); only the walk's last stop falls back to the old "found its own way round". A stop more than 48 blocks off first gets a planned way (`life/Route`, an A* over the world plan in 8 to 20 block cells, off the server thread, about 30 ms): it keeps out of the lake, rivers and open water, never climbs a planned cliff, and prefers the verge beside a Thunderpath (the road itself is only crossed); a camp sunk in a hollow is routed to as near as the plan allows. Checked on three seeds: every kittypet and loner route to every camp gate is found, with almost no water against about 300 samples on the straight line. The cat waits for a player behind it on the way (not the straight line), walks back to one more than 22 blocks off, and is only ever moved where the player cannot see it go or arrive (`Lead.put`); when the player is 72 blocks off and out of sight it is brought to their side on their own level, never onto a wall or a cliff top beside them. Kiki saw guides vanish and reappear (2026-10-18). A stop counts once both are there. The lead ends on logout, death, a change of dimension, the cat dying or unloading, or a player who keeps going the other way. The cat wears the busy tag while leading and gets its own mode, wander centre and home back afterwards. A server stop puts every led cat back. A cat that loads still marked as led (after a crash) is put back too, and a guide whose walk was cut short has gone home (it is removed). `LeadGameTests` checks the order of stops, the waiting, the walk round a wall through its gap (never over it, never put ahead), the release of the cat, one player per cat, and the kittypet's choices, journal entry and lake-free route to every camp.
- **Keepsakes** (`life/Keepsakes`): from the start of their story a cat always carries StarClan's Guidance, the Clan journal, claws and whiskers. A death keeps each in its own slot (nothing drops, nothing to pick up again), and one that goes missing anyway (lost over a restart while its owner lay dead) is back within two seconds. Since a missing one comes back, none can leave any other way: a tossed one stays in the paw it was tossed from, and one put in a chest or other container comes back to the inventory when the screen closes. Tossed claws used to lie on the ground while new ones appeared, a duplication (playtest 2026-10-19). Kiki lost her books to a death and had no leather for new claws (2026-10-18). `KeepsakesGameTests` checks they are given once, kept through a death, and kept back when tossed.
- **The Clan map** (`life/rpg/ClanMaps`, `client/MapPainter`, `client/ClanMapScreen`): the journal's Map bookmark (key 6) shows the lake, the old forest or the whole Great Journey, drawn from the world's layout rather than explored chunks: territories and areas in colour with a key, the area names (ThunderClan, Twolegplace, Tallpines, Highstones, the mountains, the sea...), every camp in its Clan's colour, the books' places as diamonds (Fourtrees, Sunningrocks, the Moonpool, Midnight's sett...), the Thunderpaths, on the journey overview the dotted way the Clans walked, the player (red, with a line for where they face) and their goal (a gold star at the edge when it lies off the map). The goal is the Campaign chapter's place, else the first place a task asks for. Click the map for the full-screen map with every place named; hover any marker for its name. Built once per seed (160 x 160 cells, cropped to the land) and refreshed once a second while open, so the player's marker follows them. Asked for by the owner, 2026-11-15. `ClanMapGameTests` checks three seeds and writes previews to `build/verification/clanlands-maps/clan-map-*.png`.

## Camp life
`life/events/CampRoutine` gives each camp a day while a player is within 128 blocks of it (`campRoutine` and `campRoutineRange` in `clanlands-events-server.toml`). It runs in the camps where the Clans live now (`Homeland`): in the Campaign the old forest's through acts 1 and 2, none while the Clans are away on the Great Journey, then the lake's. At dawn the deputy leads a border patrol of two or three cats out through the way in; a morning hunt, an afternoon patrol (a hunt in leaf-bare) and, in leaf-bare and every other day, a late hunt follow, and the medicine cat goes out for herbs on dry mornings and afternoons. Outings walk the whole way in point by point (`CampSite` lays it out from the camp plan: the hedge gap, the ravine's or quarry's cut, the island's ford), linger at one or two spots in the Clan's own land, and come back the same way; hunters carry Clan-fitting prey (fish for RiverClan, rabbit on the moor, frogs in ShadowClan's marsh) that Warrior Cats Events' own goal drops on the fresh-kill pile. In camp, cats eat from the pile, share tongues in pairs, sun by the great rock, nap at their nests and idle at their dens; kits play by the nursery with a moss ball while a queen sits watching, apprentices spar with a warrior, the medicine cat visits the dens, elders rest by theirs, the leader sits watching the camp. Rain keeps the camp near its dens, and there are no outings in a thunderstorm. After sunset every cat walks to its own nest and sleeps until its own dawn, and one or two warriors guard the inside of the way in, pacing from side to side. Each cat does one thing at a time with its own end. The routine walks a cat in WCE's FOLLOW mode (no owner, so WCE's own wandering, patrols and homing are off) and lingers in WANDER with WCE's chilling and resting poses or in SIT; the `greatjourney_routine` tag marks a cat it holds out of WANDER, so one left so by a restart is given back. It never steers a cat with the busy tag, one in a Clan event, the lost kit, a cat on WCE's own patrol or called by a leader, a player's own cat, or one a player has just given an order (left alone ten minutes); a Clan's big event pauses its camp. A walk that stops getting nearer for ten seconds hops the last stretch only where no player is within 24 blocks (along the same route, so never over the barrier), else the activity is given up. Nothing reads an unloaded chunk. `CampRoutineGameTests` runs a drawn camp through a day (varied activities, patrols, herbs, play, sparring), a night (every cat at its nest asleep, one or two guards, awake by mid-morning), checks that busy and event cats are left alone and that outings cross the barrier only between neighbouring points of the way in, and lays out every lake and old-forest camp's way in on four seeds (an island whose ford reaches no dry bank within 80 blocks, as seed 777001's forest RiverClan, keeps its cats home).

## Guided tour
`/greatjourney tour` (anyone; `tour stop` ends it) plays a self-running tour of the world in the books' order: the old forest, the Great Journey, then the lake. The player is made a spectator with night vision (`life/tour/Tour`); the server carries them from stop to stop so the land loads, and their own client flies the camera and shows the captions (it needs the mod on the client). Their place, game mode and facing are saved first and put back when the tour ends, is stopped, or on the next login after leaving mid-tour. A tour that hears nothing from its client for 75 seconds ends.

Stops come from the running world's own layout (`TourStops`), so a place the world lacks is left out: Fourtrees, the ThunderClan camp, Sunningrocks and the stepping stones, the RiverClan camp, the Thunderpath (a monster drives past), ShadowClan and Carrionplace, the WindClan moor, the gorge, the tunnels, Mothermouth, Barley's farm; then the Great Thunderpath, the Tribe's cave, Midnight's sett, the first sight of the lake; then the island, RiverClan's marsh, the stone hollow, WindClan's hills, ShadowClan's pines, the Moonpool and the Twoleg nest.

## Monsters on the Thunderpaths (Traffic)
`life/traffic/` sends Twoleg "monsters", cars and long trucks, along the roads. They are not mobs: each is a set of block displays (painted body, glass, wheels, lamps) moved together. About once a second, each stretch of road with a cat (player or Clan cat) within 28 blocks of its edge may roll a monster, which starts out of sight, growls closer, roars past and is gone. The great highway is busy, the forest Thunderpaths now and then, the old Thunderpath never. Monsters hurt what they hit (14 damage, seven hearts, by default), scare Clan cats off the road, keep a gap behind slower ones, and are never saved (leftovers from a crash are removed on load). Settings are in the per-world `serverconfig/clanlands-traffic-server.toml`: `enabled`, `thunderpathChance` (1/12), `highwayChance` (1/3), `damage`, `maxMonsters` (24), `maxPerRoad` (6). Ops can use `/greatjourney monster spawn|spawnat|count|clear`.

## Checking a layout
- `./gradlew :clanlands:runVerification` draws each test seed's plans to `mods/clanlands/build/verification/clanlands-maps/`.
- `tools/full_pack_server.py --seed N --command "forceload add ..." --hold 600` generates real chunks.
- `tools/render_region_map.py build/server-harness/world/region out.png` shows what actually generated.
- `tools/render_slice.py <region> out.png --through X Z` cuts a side view along the stream at X Z: the way to check a waterfall.
- `tools/scan_region.py <region> --box X0 Z0 X1 Z1` lists lava open to the air, water standing over air or with an open side, and chunk-border seams.
- `tools/inspect_world.py` boots the full pack headless on a few seeds (Free, plus a Campaign boot that checks only the spawn), forceloads every landmark, then writes `build/inspect/<run-id>/report.html` with a top-down tile per landmark, side slices (camps through their way in, waterfalls, bridges, culverts, tunnel mouths) and automatic checks: one way out of each camp, the island cut off by water but walkable over the tree-bridge, tunnels reaching the cavern, a column of water at each fall, roads and bridges, and a standable spawn. `--reuse --run-id R` re-analyses worlds already generated. Never point it at the hub.
- `tools/playtest.py --suite smoke|free-main|free-starts|campaign|mp` plays real full-pack clients in the background (the `mp` suite uses a dedicated server and two clients) and writes `build/playtest/<run>/report.html`; `--report DIR` only rebuilds a report.
- `tools/gradle_locked.py :clanlands:runVerification` runs Gradle through a lock, so helpers sharing the tree never build at once.
