# Changelog

## 0.1.5

- **A lost mentor is always replaced.** If your mentor dies, leaves the Clan or retires, you are told at once wherever you are, and the leader names a new mentor as soon as you are back in camp. What you had already done of the step carries over. In the Campaign, your mentor is still your mentor after the Clans settle at the lake.
- **An apprentice of your own.** A warrior or a full medicine cat is given one of the Clan's apprentices. Ask them along, teach them four lessons, and the leader gives them their warrior name. A medicine cat names their own apprentice.
- **Deputy and leader.** Train an apprentice and earn the Clan's trust, and your leader can make you deputy. A deputy leads the Clan when the leader's last life is gone, or when the old leader steps down for them. A new leader walks to the Moonpool for nine lives and a "-star" name, and chooses a deputy by talking to a warrior. The journal says what the next step needs.
- **Found a Clan of your own (Free Mode).** A loner can win over wild cats, lead three of them to land no Clan has marked, and found a Clan there. The journal shows what the new Clan still needs: a camp, six cats, a deputy and a medicine cat. Then its founder receives nine lives at the Moonpool, and the four Clans hear of it at their next Gathering.
- **Your warrior name is yours to choose.** At your naming ceremony you can keep the name you chose when you made your cat, take one of four your Clan offers, or ask for other names as often as you like.
- **Existing worlds load as they are.** Checked against worlds saved by 0.1.4: every cat keeps their mentor, their step and their standing.

## 0.1.4

- **Many more warrior names.** Warrior names now end in one of 231 endings in the books' style (-pelt, -stripe, -frost, -flight, -whisker, -fang, -song, -blossom, -pool and many more), so the Clans can have tens of thousands of different warriors. Your own warrior ceremony offers four new endings in every world, and no name uses the same word twice (no "Firefire").

## 0.1.3

- **Worlds keep opening.** Three worldgen mods together made the world's saved terrain settings grow every time the world was opened: one part tripled, until after about a dozen opens the world refused to load ("Tried to read NBT tag that was too big"); another added a layer each time, slowing new terrain a little more on every open. Both now stay the same size, and a world already hit by this opens again and shrinks back on its next save.

## 0.1.2

- **Slower seasons.** Each season now lasts 72 days instead of 24, and the leaves change colour every 24 days instead of every 8.

## 0.1.1: the first release

Fixes from the family playtest, on top of everything below:

- **Act 2 reaches the sea.** Walking anywhere onto the beach where the sun drowns (or to Midnight's sett) now counts, the journal and a reminder tell you how far and which way the sea is, and StarClan's sign comes after a few days even if you were away from the forest.
- **Mentors are shared out.** A new apprentice, or one whose mentor has gone to StarClan, gets a warrior who has no apprentice yet whenever the Clan has one.
- **Livelier camps.** Each cat does a few different things through the day, and the morning hunting patrol no longer gets skipped on busy days.

## 0.1.0

The Great Journey: live as a Warrior Cat among the four Clans, from the old forest to the lake.

- **Two ways to play.** Campaign follows the books: life in the old forest, the Twolegs tearing it down, StarClan's sign, Midnight by the sea where the sun drowns, the Great Journey over the mountains, and the new home by the lake. Free Mode drops you into Clan life by the lake.
- **The Clan lands.** Every world builds the books' places from its seed: Fourtrees, the four camps, Sunningrocks, Highstones, Twolegplace, the Thunderpath, the island, the Moonpool, the Tribe's cave behind the waterfall, and more.
- **The Clan map.** Open your Clan Journal (J) and turn to the Map bookmark. It shows the lake, the old forest or the whole Great Journey, with every camp and place named, a key, where you are, and a gold star on where your story is sending you. Click the map to see it big.
- **Clan life.** Apprentice and warrior duties, medicine cat work, patrols, Gatherings, ceremonies, scars, seasons and Clan news.
- **A dedicated server pack** is attached to this file: unzip it, run install, then start.
